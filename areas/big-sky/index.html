Mountain living. Everyday comfort.

A mountain home should work beautifully when you’re actually living in it. Places for boots and gear, comfortable gathering spaces, useful storage, and finishes that suit your routines deserve just as much attention as the view.

CT Custom Builders serves Big Sky with custom home, remodeling, and general contracting services. We begin with your project goals and the property itself: access, site conditions, available information, and the scope you have in mind.

If you’ll be coordinating the project from elsewhere, tell us early. Discussing decision-making, site visits, and how information will be shared helps set expectations before work begins.

A thoughtful starting point

Plan for the way you use your mountain home.

Start at the entry.

Think about wet boots, coats, equipment, and guests arriving together. Practical entries and storage can make a big difference to daily comfort.

Consider the site early.

Ask about access, delivery logistics, and how site conditions affect the scope. Timing should be discussed in the context of the specific property.

Make room for good communication.

If you live out of town, share how often you expect to visit and how you prefer to review decisions. Agree on a workable approach together.

Before we build

A few good questions.

Good projects begin with clear expectations. Here’s where to start.

Can we discuss a second home in Big Sky?
Yes. Tell us how you expect to use the home, where the property is located, and whether you’re planning new construction or improvements to an existing home.
I don’t live locally. Can I still start a project conversation?
Yes. Email your project details and let us know where you are based. We can discuss communication, site visits, and decision-making as part of the planning process.
How is a Big Sky construction schedule determined?
The project scope, design readiness, site access, material availability, and seasonal conditions all need consideration. A schedule is discussed after the specifics of your property and project are understood.
