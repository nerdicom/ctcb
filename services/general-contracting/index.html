Bring the moving parts together.

A building project involves more than the work you see on site. There are plans to review, materials to select, trades to coordinate, and decisions that affect everything that follows.

CT Custom Builders approaches general contracting with an emphasis on understanding the scope before construction begins. We want to know the intended result, the condition of the property, the people involved, and the constraints that matter to you.

From new residential construction to remodeling, a useful plan sets out responsibilities, sequencing, and how changes will be discussed. Bring your current drawings, project notes, and questions so we can look at the work as a whole.

A thoughtful starting point

Clarity before construction.

Define the work.

A project description and available drawings help establish what is included, what still needs design, and which decisions remain open.

Understand the sequence.

Material availability, site access, and the order of trades all influence a project. Discuss timing before treating any date as a commitment.

Keep decisions connected.

A change to one part of a project can affect cost, materials, and timing elsewhere. Review those connections before agreeing to a new direction.

Before we build

A few good questions.

Good projects begin with clear expectations. Here’s where to start.

What information should I send for a new project?
Start with your name, project location, type of work, preferred timing, and a short description. Include drawings or photos if you have them.
Do you work on new construction and renovations?
Yes. We welcome residential construction, remodeling, and addition inquiries. Project fit and availability are discussed once we understand the location and scope.
What happens if the scope changes?
Changes should be discussed in terms of the work, cost, and schedule before proceeding. The project agreement establishes the process for documenting and approving changes.
