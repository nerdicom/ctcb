More than a floor plan.

A custom home is a chance to get the everyday things right: the light in the kitchen, a place for muddy boots, space to gather, and a quiet room at the end of the day. CT Custom Builders starts with those priorities.

Whether you’re exploring a property or already working from architectural drawings, the first conversation is about the whole project. Site conditions, access, layout, materials, budget, and timing all deserve a place in the plan.

Our custom home conversations cover the decisions that connect a design to a buildable scope. If you have an architect, designer, or engineer involved, let us know so the right people can be part of the discussion.

  • Site and project goals
  • Layout and everyday functionality
  • Materials, finishes, and priorities
  • Construction scope and sequencing

A thoughtful starting point

A little preparation goes a long way.

Start with the property.

Share the location, any available site information, and what you know about access and utilities. A home should respond to its setting from the beginning.

Name your must-haves.

Think about the rooms you use most, storage, natural light, and how your household might change. A short list of priorities helps guide tradeoffs.

Bring your team and ideas.

Plans, sketches, inspiration photos, and an early budget range are all useful. You don’t need a finished design to begin a conversation.

Before we build

A few good questions.

Good projects begin with clear expectations. Here’s where to start.

Can I contact you before I have a floor plan?
Yes. Tell us where you’d like to build, what you’re imagining, and where you are in the process. Early conversations help identify the questions that need answers before construction.
Can I bring plans from my architect?
Yes. Share your plans and let us know who is on your design team. The next step is to review the intended scope, site information, and construction expectations together.
Do you publish a standard price per square foot?
No. Site work, design, materials, systems, and finish selections can change the cost substantially. A useful budget starts with the details of your own project.
