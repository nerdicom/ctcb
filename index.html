Before we build

A few good questions.

Good projects begin with clear expectations. Here’s where to start.

What areas do you serve?
CT Custom Builders serves Bozeman, Big Sky, and the Gallatin Valley in Montana. Email us your project location so we can discuss access, scope, and scheduling.
What types of projects can we talk about?
We welcome conversations about custom homes, kitchen and bathroom remodels, whole-home renovations, additions, and general contracting. Start with your goals, and we can work through the right scope for your property.
Do I need finished plans before reaching out?
No. You can start with an idea, inspiration images, or preliminary drawings. If you already have an architect or a complete set of plans, include that information when you get in touch.
How much will my project cost, and how long will it take?
Every project has its own scope, site conditions, materials, and schedule. We’ll need to understand those details before discussing a realistic budget and timeline. A short project description is a good first step.
